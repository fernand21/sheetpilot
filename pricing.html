Frequently asked questions

Are payments automatic?

No. Paid plans renew manually each month. You make the payment and the renewal is confirmed.

What happens if I renew late?

You have a 24-hour grace period after the due date. After that, paid capacity may pause until renewal is confirmed.

What counts as a request?

Each request to a published API counts once. CORS preflight requests do not consume quota. Counters reset at the start of each UTC month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Changing a plan changes the configured capacity, not the URL of your API.

Do API consumers need a LittleAPI account?

No. Public reads work without a LittleAPI account. Only the owner signs in to manage APIs and usage.
